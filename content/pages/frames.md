# The Frames

This page is a draft placeholder.

The four frames are four lenses for looking at art. The same artwork will
yield different readings through each frame, and a strong HSC response
typically integrates more than one.

> ⚠️  This page is `status: draft`. Body content to be authored.

- **Subjective frame** — what the work feels like; the psychological, the personal, the intuitive.
- **Cultural frame** — how the work engages class, race, gender, ethnicity, politics; the social and historical conditions.
- **Structural frame** — what the work is made of; how signs, symbols, materials, composition and convention construct meaning.
- **Postmodern frame** — how the work appropriates, recontextualises, parodies, blurs categories, and questions originality.
