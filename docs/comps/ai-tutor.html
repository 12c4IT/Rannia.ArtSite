Prompt · 01

Paragraph feedback

Paste your paragraph. The AI reads it against HSC marking criteria and suggests one specific improvement.

You are helping me practise for HSC Visual Arts. I will paste one paragraph of a short-answer response. Read it against NESA marking criteria for [5 / 8 / 10]-mark responses. Comment on: 1. Which frame(s) I appear to be using and whether I've named them. 2. Whether I analyse or only describe. 3. One specific improvement I could make in a rewrite. Do not rewrite my paragraph for me. Guidance, not marking. My paragraph: [paste here]
Works in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity.
Prompt · 02

BOW photo critique

Attach a photo of your BOW. The AI critiques composition, technique, and concept in that order. Not marking — guidance.

You are helping me practise for HSC Visual Arts. I have attached a photo of one work from my Body of Work. My conceptual intent is: [one or two sentences] Give me a critique in three sections, in this order: 1. Composition — what's working, what's fighting. 2. Technique — what the surface / material / process is doing. 3. Concept — how well the work is delivering the intent I stated. End with one specific thing I could try in the next iteration. Remind me this is guidance, not marking.
Use a tool that accepts image uploads.
Prompt · 03

Practice question generator

Generates fresh practice questions tailored to a specific artist, frame, or content area. Doesn't give you the answer.

You are helping me practise for HSC Visual Arts. Generate one [5 / 8 / 10]-mark short-answer question in NESA style about: - Artist: [e.g. Louise Bourgeois] - Frame focus: [e.g. cultural] - Content area (optional): [e.g. the body] Do not give me the answer. After I respond, tell me where my response would land in the marking rubric and suggest one specific improvement.
Great for daily drills.
Prompt · 04

Band 6 scaffold builder

Walks you through building a Band 6 response step by step. Each step is a fresh prompt to you.

You are helping me build a Band 6 HSC Visual Arts extended response, step by step. I will give you the question and my case study. Guide me through, one step at a time, waiting for my answer before continuing. Question: [paste here] Case study I'll anchor the response in: [artist name] Steps in order: 1. Decode the verbs and key terms in the question. 2. Choose the frames most useful for this question. 3. Draft a thesis sentence — I write it, you critique it. 4. Plan three paragraphs (topic sentence + one artwork citation each). 5. Draft one paragraph — I write it, you critique it. Guidance, not marking.
Great as an exam-week routine.
