Scaffold Try it first

Decode the question

  • Analyse — break into parts and show how they relate.
  • Materials and processes — the physical means and the making, not the subject.
  • Shape meaning — argue the causal link, don't merely describe.

Plan

  • Intro: thesis — materials carry meaning; nominate a frame and name your artist(s).
  • Body 1 & 2: one work each; material choice → reading, with cited evidence.
  • Conclusion: return to the thesis; the choice is the content.

Sentence stems

“By casting the legs in visibly-seamed bronze, the artist …”

“The shift from marble to latex registers as a shift from … to …”

“Read through the subjective frame, this material choice …”

Marker's view

Band 6: a sustained argument that integrates the frame with close material analysis, precise vocabulary, and cited evidence across two works.

Band 4: materials and frame named, but description outweighs analysis and the causal link stays implicit.

Exemplar structure

A skeleton, not a model essay: thesis → Bourgeois Maman (bronze/marble/steel, scale, tension) → Bourgeois The Destruction of the Father (latex, red light, the room as fantasy) → synthesis. Two works, one argument.
