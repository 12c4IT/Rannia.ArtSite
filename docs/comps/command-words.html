Read the verb. It tells you what to do.

NESA marking is verb-driven. "Analyse" is not "describe". "Evaluate" is not "explain". Each command word below has a NESA definition, a plain-English gloss, and a worked example paragraph so you can see the verb in action.

Evaluate

Discuss

Identify issues and provide points for and/or against. Move around the argument.

Account for = state reasons for, report on. Give an account of = narrate a series of events.

Justify

Support an argument or conclusion. Show why your position is defensible.

Compare

Show how things are similar or different. Both similarities and differences.

Assess

Make a judgement of value, quality, outcomes, results, or size. Like "evaluate" — but softer.

Worked example

What it looks like to actually analyse

One question. One Band 6 paragraph. One marker's note. Bookmark this and come back when the verb changes.

Analyse
ALARMS · A — Analyse

Identify components and the relationship between them; draw out and relate implications.

In plain English Break the work into parts. Say what each part is doing. Then say how the parts work on each other. It's not "describe" — description alone won't reach the mark.
The question

Analyse how one artist you have studied uses scale to make meaning.

8 marks
Band 6 response

Louise Bourgeois uses scale in Maman (1999) as a compositional device that produces meaning through the viewer's body. The spider stands 9.27 metres tall1: a scale that forces adults to stand under her, in the position of a small child. The relationship Bourgeois analyses through scale is therefore not just formal — it is relational. The viewer becomes a subordinate figure inside a maternal architecture.

This scale also transforms the material. Cast bronze at monumental size reads not as an insect but as a shelter: a hollow underside carrying twenty-six eggs1. The shift from small (real spider) to monumental (public sculpture) is what allows Bourgeois to move the work from personal biography into public reception, where audiences read it as an homage to her mother.

Marker's note

The response earns the mark because it doesn't stop at description. It names a component (scale), says what that component does (forces a bodily position on the viewer), and then relates that back to meaning (the viewer becomes subordinate to a maternal architecture). That third move is where "analyse" lives.
