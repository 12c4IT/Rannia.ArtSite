---
term: "Explain"
slug: explain
alarmsRung: explain

definition: |
  Relate cause and effect; make the relationship between things evident; provide reasons for why and/or how.

studentGloss: |
  Say what caused what. Show the reader the mechanism. "Why?" and "how?" are the two questions your response has to answer. It's a step beyond *describe* — you're not just naming what's there, you're saying what makes it work.

workedExample:
  question: "Explain how one artist you have studied uses material choices to comment on the world they were formed by."
  markValue: 8
  response: |
    Louise Bourgeois' material choices are inseparable from the world of her childhood. She grew up in a household organised around tapestry restoration — her mother's craft — and her practice returns repeatedly to weaving, sewing, and fabric. In *The Destruction of the Father* (1974), Bourgeois works in latex, a material with no fine-art tradition in 1974, borrowed from prosthetics and medical use. The choice reads as a rejection of the male-dominated sculptural materials of her generation (steel, welded iron of the minimalists) in favour of a material culturally coded as soft, medical, and bodily.

    In *Maman* (1999), by contrast, Bourgeois returns to bronze — the most orthodox material in Western public sculpture. The choice is strategic, not conservative. By working in the material of civic monuments, Bourgeois inserts the maternal — a subject that art history has kept small and domestic — at the scale of the civic bronze. The material *explains* the argument: the world she was formed by kept the maternal private, so her sculpture makes it monumental.
  markersNote: |
    "Explain" wants cause and effect. This response gives it: material choice (latex, then bronze) → the world that made those materials mean what they mean (medical, then civic) → how Bourgeois' use argues against that inheritance. Students who stay at the level of "what the material is" land Band 3–4. This response earns higher by treating material as *cultural* choice with consequences.
