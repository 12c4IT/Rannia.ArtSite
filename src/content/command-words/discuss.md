---
term: "Discuss"
slug: discuss
alarmsRung: analyse

definition: |
  Identify issues and provide points for and/or against. Move across the argument, not just around one position.

studentGloss: |
  Move. A "discuss" response is not a lecture — it argues with itself. Name the issue, put forward one side, then bring in the counter-view, then land your position. You don't have to be neutral; you do have to acknowledge the other side.

workedExample:
  question: "Discuss how the postmodern frame changes what we can see in a work by an artist you have studied."
  markValue: 8
  response: |
    Read through the postmodern frame, Louise Bourgeois' *Maman* (1999) becomes visible as a citation — a knowing quotation of every monumental bronze animal in Western public art history, from Rome's Capitoline she-wolf onward. What the subjective frame reads as a personal memorial to Bourgeois' mother, the postmodern frame reads as a deliberate re-inscription of a masculine sculptural tradition. Bourgeois takes the form of the civic bronze monument (heroic, permanent, male) and inserts a mother into it.

    However, a purely postmodern reading also flattens something. The subjective claim Bourgeois herself made — that the spider *is* her mother — is not just biographical noise the postmodern frame can dispense with. Bourgeois' interviews return again and again to the emotional register: the spider as protector, as craftsperson, as absence. If we read *Maman* only as a citation, we lose the psychological force the work has for viewers who meet it before they know its history.

    The postmodern frame is therefore best used *alongside* the subjective frame in Bourgeois' case, not instead of it. It makes visible the citational strategy that the subjective frame ignores; the subjective frame makes visible the affective content that a citational reading flattens.
  markersNote: |
    "Discuss" asks for movement — arguments on more than one side. This response earns the mark by naming both what the postmodern frame reveals AND what it flattens, then landing a position (use both frames). A response that only claims the postmodern frame is illuminating, without acknowledging its costs, would land in the middle band.
