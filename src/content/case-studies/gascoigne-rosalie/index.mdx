## Artist practice

{/* AI DRAFT — Rannia to rewrite. */}

Rosalie Gascoigne's practice is unusual for its late start (first solo exhibition at 57), its consistency (a single sustained investigation of the Monaro landscape across two decades), and its material discipline (found regional material only — grass, wooden soft-drink crates, retro-reflective road signs, torn paper from a specific weathered kiosk). She trained in ikebana in Canberra for two decades before beginning to exhibit; the arrangement principles of that training are legible in every mature work.
