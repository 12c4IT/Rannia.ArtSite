## Artist practice



Yasumasa Morimura's practice is remarkably singular across four decades: appropriated self-portraits into the images he studied as a Japanese art student. The images have shifted — European Old Masters, Hollywood actresses, 20th-century political photographs — but the method is consistent. Costume, makeup, set reconstruction, physical performance for the camera. Morimura almost never uses digital compositing; the transformations are staged as physical events.
