## Artist practice



Tracey Moffatt's practice moves fluently between photography, film and video, and her formal choices are always chosen for what they can hold. Cibachrome saturation for melodrama; offset monochrome for documentary; single-channel video for the domestic scenes of *Bedevil* (1993). She does not have a single medium; she has a set of media that she selects between according to which visual register her subject requires.
