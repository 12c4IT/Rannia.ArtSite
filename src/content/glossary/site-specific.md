---
term: "Site-specific"
slug: site-specific
shortDefinition: |
  Art made for — and often unable to exist meaningfully outside of — a particular location. The work's meaning is partly produced by where it stands. Common in installation, land art, and public sculpture (e.g. Rosalie Gascoigne's roadside-material assemblages engage a specifically Australian landscape).
