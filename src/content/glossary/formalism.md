---
term: "Formalism"
slug: formalism
shortDefinition: |
  A critical approach that reads an artwork through its formal properties — colour, line, composition, material — rather than through its subject matter or context. Formalism was dominant in mid-twentieth-century American criticism (Clement Greenberg) and is still the analytical mode most associated with the structural frame.
