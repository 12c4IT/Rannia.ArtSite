shortDefinition: |
  A pre-existing manufactured object presented as a work of art — the term is Marcel Duchamp's, from 1913 onward (the *Bicycle Wheel*, the urinal titled *Fountain*). The gesture is a foundational move for both conceptual art and the postmodern frame.
