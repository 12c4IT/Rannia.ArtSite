## What the subjective frame is

The subjective frame reads a work through **feeling, intuition, memory, and psychology**. It asks how a work operates on the individual viewer — the emotional, imaginative, and personal responses it produces — and how the artist's own biography, psychology, and life experience shape the work's meaning.

## Questions this frame asks

- What emotional response does the work provoke, and how?
- What in the artist's biography or psychology gives rise to this work?
- What personal memories, associations, or dreams does the work draw on?
- How does the work speak to the individual viewer's inner life?
