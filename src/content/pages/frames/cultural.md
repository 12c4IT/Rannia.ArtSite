## What the cultural frame is

The cultural frame reads a work through **the social conditions in which it was made and received**: class, race, gender, ethnicity, sexuality, religion, politics, and the historical moment. It treats art as never separable from the world that produced it, and asks how meaning is negotiated between artist, work, and society.

## Questions this frame asks

- What social, political, or historical conditions produced this work?
- Whose voice is speaking, and whose voice is absent?
- What cultural values does the work embody, contest, or transform?
- Who is the audience — and who is excluded?
