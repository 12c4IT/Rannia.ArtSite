## About this site

This is a study resource for NSW Stage 6 Visual Arts (Preliminary and HSC), authored by Rannia. Every case study is verified against cited sources before publication. Every image is rights-cleared. No unsourced AI-generated content is ever published.

## Editorial policy

- **Sources.** Every factual claim on this site cites a source. If we can't find a reliable source, we don't publish the claim.
- **Quotes.** Verbatim, attributed, with a page number or URL.
- **HSC questions.** Transcribed verbatim from NESA past papers. Never paraphrased.
- **Images.** Used only under public-domain, Creative Commons, institutional educational-use, or Australian fair-dealing for research/study — with full attribution and minimum necessary resolution.
- **Last reviewed.** Every case study shows when it was last reviewed and by whom.
- **Draft vs. published.** Every entry has a status. Only entries marked `published` appear on the live site.

## Report a concern

If you're an artist, gallery, or rights-holder and something on this site needs correction, attribution changed, or removed, contact us via the footer link and we'll respond within 48 hours during term time.

## AI disclosure

Content on this site is authored by Rannia with editorial assistance from AI tooling. Every claim is verified against the cited sources before publication. Nothing is published without her review.
